About

I'm Kelly Tall and I am research and insights specialist with more than 20 years’ experience across analysis, research, media, academia, data visualisation and storytelling. I started out in research and insights, working with respected brands such as David Jones, Coca-Cola and Hardy's. I moved into analysis, insights and data visualisation design about sixteen years ago.

I have a Master of Data Science and Innovation from UTS, where my focus was on data visualisation and data ethics. I currently work as a Senior Manager of data visualisation at CBA where I analyse and translate data and findings into clear data graphics and commentary for the CBA Board/Audit Committee and Senior Leadership.
