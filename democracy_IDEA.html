Established democracies are under pressure too

While the most dramatic declines tend to occur in already-fragile states, established democracies are not immune. Many countries long considered democratic exemplars have seen measurable erosion across key indicators — from press freedom and judicial independence to civil liberties and credible elections. The data suggests democratic backsliding is a global phenomenon, not confined to any one region.

United States

Old2024
The United States shows a pronounced pattern of democratic erosion, with declines visible across fundamental rights, rule of law, and credible elections over the past decade. From a historically strong baseline, the trajectory raises questions about the resilience of its democratic institutions in the years ahead.
